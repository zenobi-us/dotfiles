## Review rules

- Make sure that important reveals have evidence or setup.
- Make sure that secrets affect behavior before disclosure.
- Make sure that a reveal changes a goal, relationship, available option, or cost.
- Distinguish a missing fact from a contradiction.
- Record flashbacks, recordings, lies, and unreliable accounts explicitly.
- Keep reader knowledge separate from character knowledge.
- Do not resolve a promise only with explanation. Show its consequence.

A reveal can confirm, reverse, or complicate an expectation. It must not remove tension without creating new pressure.
