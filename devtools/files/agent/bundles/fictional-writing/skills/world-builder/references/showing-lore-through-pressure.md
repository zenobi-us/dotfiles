# Showing Lore Through Pressure

Use setting facts through character action. Do not stop the story for a catalogue of setting information.

## Rules

- Give each setting fact a story use.
- Show history through a present consequence.
- Show technology through access, cost, failure, or misuse.
- Show institutions through rules and effects.
- Show social conditions through choices and limits.
- Introduce names when characters need to act on them.
- Explain only the part that affects the current scene.
- Let characters disagree about what a fact means.

For each lore detail, answer:

1. Who needs this information?
2. What action does it affect?
3. What pressure does it create?
4. What remains unknown?

Replace a lore paragraph with a choice, obstacle, transaction, argument, or consequence when possible.

## Router trigger

Use this reference when a setting needs explanation, or when a draft contains lore dumps or names that do not affect the story.
