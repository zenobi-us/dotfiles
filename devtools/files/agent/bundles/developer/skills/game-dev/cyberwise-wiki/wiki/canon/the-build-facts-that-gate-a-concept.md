# Five attributes, an attribute floor, and the cyberware a stealth concept actually depends on

Character concepts arrive as fiction and have to survive contact with a build.
Three facts do most of the gatekeeping.

## The game has five attributes

**Body, Intelligence, Reflexes, Technical Ability, Cool.** Five, not six.

The common error is counting the Relic progression as a sixth. It is a **skill
tree**, not an attribute - it does not take attribute points and it does not gate
what the five gate. Anyone describing a character "with six attributes" has
merged the two.

Each attribute is also a personality handle, which is why this belongs in a canon
area at all rather than only in a build guide. A Cool-heavy V and a Body-heavy V
are different people before they are different stat lines, and asking a player
which two their V leans on is a faster route into the character than asking about
their childhood.

## Dumping an attribute to zero is not a build the game offers

**Every attribute starts at 3 and cannot be lowered.** There is no zero. So the
question "can I play a V with no Body at all" has a flat answer before any
balance discussion: the character creator will not let you.

The reason it would be unplayable if it did is worth knowing anyway, because it
tells you what minimising Body actually costs:

- **Health comes off Body.** At the bottom, V dies effectively instantly.
- **Carry capacity comes off Body.** V cannot pick loot up, which breaks the
  economy the entire game runs on.

So the honest answer to a player proposing a physically frail V is: **the floor
makes it legal and the two derived stats make it miserable.** That is a real
answer with a real trade-off in it, and it is a much better conversation than
"you can't."

## Optical Camo, and why it is a Cool question

**Optical Camo occupies the integumentary system slot.** It grants a few seconds
of cloaking on a cooldown.

The part that decides whether it carries a concept is where it plugs in: it is
**hooked into the Cool tree**, where perks can auto-activate it or use it to break
line of sight. Bare, it is a short panic button. Supported by the tree, it is the
spine of a stealth build.

The general shape is worth holding: **cyberware is rarely the whole answer to
"can my V do X" - the tree behind it usually is.** A player describing a V who
vanishes is describing a Cool investment, not a shopping trip to a ripperdoc, and
answering at the item level alone gets them a build that does not do what they
pictured.

Pair this with
[Only power weapons take a muzzle](/canon/muzzle-slots-and-suppression): a stealth
concept usually needs both the camo and a weapon class that can actually be quiet,
and the second one catches people out far more often.

## Scope

Read off the character sheet and cyberware screens on patch 2.31. The attribute
list and the floor are structural. Perk specifics inside the Cool tree move
between patches - re-check those rather than the shape.
