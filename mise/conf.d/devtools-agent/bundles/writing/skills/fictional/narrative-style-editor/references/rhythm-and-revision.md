# Rhythm and Revision

Use sound and contrast to improve prose after story structure is stable.

## Rhythm review

- Read the passage aloud.
- Mark repeated sentence lengths.
- Remove accidental repetition.
- Vary sentence length when the emotion changes.
- Keep short sentences for impact.
- Keep longer sentences when the thought needs movement.
- Check that dialogue has a distinct rhythm for each speaker.

## Revision review

- Mark the words, sentences, and passages that work.
- Identify the pattern that makes them work.
- Preserve that pattern during revision.
- Revise weak passages toward the established voice.
- Remove description that does not affect mood, action, or understanding.

Do not use this reference to repair a missing story engine. Route structural problems to `story-architect` or character problems to `character-designer`.

## Router trigger

Use this reference when the structure works, but the prose or dialogue feels flat when read aloud.
