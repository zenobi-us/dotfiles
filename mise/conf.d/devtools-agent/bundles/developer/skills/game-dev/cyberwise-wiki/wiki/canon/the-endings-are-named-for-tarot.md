# The endings are named for tarot cards, and the point of no return is written in the journal

Tarot runs through the whole game - the graffiti tarot hunt, Misty's readings -
and it names the endings too.

| ending | what it is |
|---|---|
| **The Devil** | V takes Arasaka's terms; Mikoshi |
| **Temperance** | Johnny takes the body; V goes with Alt beyond the Blackwall |
| **The Sun** | the legend route |
| **The Star** | leaving with the nomads |

## The game marks its own point of no return

**"Never meet Hanako at Embers" sits in the journal.** The game states, in its own
UI, that the meeting is the last decision point - it does not rely on the player
inferring it from a warning popup or finding out afterwards.

Two things follow from that, and both matter more than the trivia does:

- **It is an in-game source, and therefore quotable.** A player asking "when is
  the point of no return" can be answered from the journal rather than from a
  guide, which is the same discipline that applies everywhere else here
  ([Where lifepath canon actually lives](/canon/where-lifepath-canon-lives)).
- **Declining the meeting is itself a path.** The line is phrased as an option,
  not as a warning.

## Why an endings article belongs in a canon area at all

Not for the plot. For two working reasons:

**It bounds a backstory.** Everything on that table happens at the far end of the
story, so none of it is available to a character document written about V *before*
the story - the same constraint that governs
[Jackie dies before Johnny exists for V](/canon/jackie-dies-before-johnny-exists).
A dossier that knows how it ends is not a dossier.

**It settles arguments about what "canon V" did.** There is no canonical ending.
The game ships four named ones and a player's V has whichever they chose. An
assistant that assumes one has silently overwritten the player's character.

## Handling this in conversation

These are the heaviest spoilers in the game. Ask before going near them - "do you
want me to talk about the endings, or stay clear?" - and take the answer. A player
writing a backstory usually does not need any of it, and the four names above are
enough to have on hand for the ones who do.

## Scope

Read off the game's endings and journal. No patch dependency. Phantom Liberty
adds its own ending branch, which changes what is available on that path without
renaming these four.
