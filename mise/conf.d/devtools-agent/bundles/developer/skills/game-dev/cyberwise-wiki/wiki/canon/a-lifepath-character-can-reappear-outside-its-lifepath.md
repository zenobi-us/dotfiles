# A streetkid-only character reappears in content every lifepath sees

The three prologues are separate; the montage that follows the first timeskip is
shared. That boundary is not as clean as it looks.

**A character who exists only in the streetkid intro appears in the shared
montage** - not named on screen there, but identifiable by his clothing - and
**later dialogue references V having beaten him up.**

## Why that is worth knowing

Two consequences, and the second is the useful one.

**For a streetkid V**, it is straightforward continuity: an encounter from the
intro is paid off twice, once visually and once in dialogue. Most players never
join the two up.

**For a corpo or nomad V**, the game is carrying a reference to an event that
character never experienced. The dialogue exists in shared content; the beating
happened in content only one lifepath played. This is what
[lifepath surviving as dialogue rather than as branches](/canon/streetkid-and-nomad-anchors)
looks like at the seams - the writers gated most of it, and a thread got through.

So when a player says "who is that, and why does V talk like they have met" - the
answer may be a lifepath they did not pick.

## The naming caution

**No name is given here on purpose.** The role is what was established: a character
from the streetkid intro, present in the montage by clothing, referenced later in
dialogue. A first name or surname attached to a minor prologue character is exactly
the class of detail that [gets supplied by community sources and traces to
nothing](/canon/the-corpo-prologue) - and a name asserted in a canon article gets
quoted back as canon within a year.

Describe the role. If a name is needed, find it in the game's own credits, subtitles
or journal first.

## Scope and confidence

`draft`. One observation, from one playthrough, joining a montage figure to an intro
character by clothing and to a later line by content. The visual identification in
particular has not been confirmed a second time, and it is the kind of thing that is
easy to be confident about and wrong about. The general point - that lifepath content
leaks into shared content - stands independently of whether this specific instance
holds up.
