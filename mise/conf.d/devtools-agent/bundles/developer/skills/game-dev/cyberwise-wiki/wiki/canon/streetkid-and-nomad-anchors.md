# The streetkid and nomad prologues give you furniture, not a biography

The corpo intro is dense with specifics - an employer, a named superior, an
operation, a documented identity. The other two are deliberately thinner. They
establish a **situation and a few pieces of furniture**, and then get out of the
way.

Knowing which is which matters, because the thin ones get padded with invented
detail far more often - there is more room to pad.

## Streetkid

The furniture is small and concrete:

- **Padre.** The Valentino fixer whose orbit V is already in.
- **The car job.** The intro's actual task, and the thing that goes sideways.
- **Jackie.** He is in this intro too, from a different direction than the corpo
  one.

That is close to all of it. Streetkid V "came back" or "never left" - the intro
puts them in Heywood's economy of favours and cars and bars, and does not
document a childhood, a family, a first crime or an age.

## Nomad

The nomad intro's one established fact is a **departure that has already
happened**: V's situation with their family has collapsed, and V rides into
Night City **alone**.

The game does not settle which clan, what the falling-out was over, whether
leaving was V's choice, or whether anyone is expecting them back. Every one of
those is a genuinely open question - and "was leaving your choice?" is the single
most productive question to ask a player building a nomad V, because the answer
changes the whole character and the game has not answered it for them.

## Why the asymmetry is a feature

**Corpos exist as records; streetkids and nomads do not.** Arasaka documents its
people because documenting people is what it does. The street keeps no files, and
a nomad family holds identity communally rather than on paper. The prologues
reflect that, and so should any document written about the character - see
[Match the document format to the world that produced the character](/canon/match-the-document-to-the-world).

The practical consequence for an assistant: **the thinner the prologue, the more
carefully you flag your own additions.** With corpo V there is an on-screen fact
to check against. With the other two there frequently is not, and a confident
sentence about streetkid V's childhood is almost certainly invention.

## All three converge

After the prologue and the timeskip, the three paths join and the main story is
the same. Lifepath survives afterwards as **dialogue options** and occasional
recognition, not as separate content. A backstory that expects the lifepath to
keep branching is describing a game that does not exist.

## Scope

Read off the streetkid and nomad intro sequences. A lifepath-expanding mod moves
these anchors; ask before assuming a player's account is wrong.
