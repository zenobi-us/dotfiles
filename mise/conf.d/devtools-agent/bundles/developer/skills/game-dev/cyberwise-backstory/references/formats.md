# Formats

> **Verified:** Cyberpunk 2077 patch 2.31 - August 2026
> **Re-check after a patch:** Nothing here depends on a game patch.

**The catalogue itself is in the base wiki, not here.** What each format *is*,
what its structure is good at, its unlock question, its skeleton and the way it
characteristically fails all live in one article in `cyberwise-wiki`'s bundle:

> `/formats/character-document-formats`

This file keeps only how to *use* the catalogue in an interview.

## How to offer a format

**The format is usually the unlock.** "Write your backstory" is a blank page and
a performance anxiety. "Write the police file on yourself" is a game with rules,
and people who swear they cannot write will fill two pages of it.

**Offer two or three, not the whole list.** Choose them from what they told you
the thing is *for*. Say in half a sentence why each might suit them, and let them
pick. They can change their mind later; nothing here is expensive to redo.

**Never hand someone a form with blanks.** The skeletons in the article are
starting shapes, not templates - turning one into a fill-in sheet turns an
interview into homework.

**Two is a legitimate answer.** A scene plus a dossier covers both halves - the
feeling and the function - and neither has to be long.

## If they cannot pick

| the state they are in | offer |
|---|---|
| an image, no plot | **one scene** |
| can talk but not write | **told to someone** |
| wants to play the character, not read about them | **play dossier** |
| shy about their own writing | **an in-world document** |
| stuck on who V *is* | **obituary** |
| one big event has to matter | **braindance** |
| likes the interview you are already doing | **interview transcript** |

**One scene is the best first format for almost anyone.** Most "I have no
backstory" turns out to mean "I have one image and no plot".

**A play dossier is the one that gets used rather than read once** - and the only
one of these a mod, an AI companion or a roleplay framework can actually consume.
If the character is being built to drive something, offer that one.
