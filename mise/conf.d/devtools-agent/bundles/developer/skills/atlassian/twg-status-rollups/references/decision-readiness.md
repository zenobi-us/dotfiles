# Decision Readiness

Use for launch/rollout readiness, milestone gates, approval asks, and other
go/no-go or proceed/hold decisions. Resolve the native project, goal, work item,
service, or decision record first. Use its identity, current update, and explicit
links as the scope boundary before widening.

Choose evidence domains from the decision rather than filling a fixed template.
Common gates include delivery and code, dependencies, decisions and approvals,
operational readiness, customer risk, and rollout/reversibility. Include
security, privacy, legal, or compliance only when the scope or connected evidence
makes them material. Missing evidence is a confidence gap, not proof of
readiness.

Use one broad connected search and at most two targeted gap searches. Hydrate no
more than five high-signal artifacts. Do not retry anchor-name synonyms or fetch
the same source through multiple projections. Use context discovery only for
material dependencies, operational health only for selected operational risks,
and responsibility routing only when approval authority or escalation changes
the decision.

Rank the gates that determine the decision. Attribute an owner or approver only
from a recorded assignment, approval, accountable role, or decision record;
otherwise name the ownership or authority gap. Give the requested decision or a
go, conditional-go, or no-go recommendation with confidence, unresolved gates,
and the conditions that would change it.
