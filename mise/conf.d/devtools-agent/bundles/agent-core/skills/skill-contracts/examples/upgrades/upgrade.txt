The installed owner.ts adapter executes this procedure for example-note/v1 to example-note/v2 only. Validate the source. Move title to document.heading and body to document.text. Retain all other authored properties and values. Preserve text exactly, including whitespace and Unicode. Validate the destination and compare normalized full authored content before persistence. A schemaVersion-only replacement is invalid because the destination requires document and forbids title and body.
