# Inspect panel selection per space

For Dotfiles users. Updated 2026-11-27.

## Result

Provide space-local panel selection and tab controls for Herdr App.

## Prerequisites

Herdr App and the supplied mods and layout.

## Steps

1. Check the shared-context-tabs mod link under ~/.config/herdr-app/mods.
2. Load the supplied layout and open a disposable tab in two spaces.
3. Choose a different panel in each space and switch between them.

## Verification

The tab strip restores each space’s panel and offers the expected tab controls.

## Recovery

Switch back to the previous layout if controls are missing; avoid closing tabs containing unsaved processes.
