# Equalise a disposable tab

For Dotfiles users. Updated 2026-11-27.

## Result

Restore equal pane dimensions after splits and closes.

## Prerequisites

Herdr App with the mod mounted, Bun available through PATH or mise, and hc.

## Steps

1. Open a disposable tab with multiple panes.
2. Resize a pane manually, then press Ctrl+Alt+E or click Equalise in the supplied tab strip.
3. Split or close a disposable pane to exercise automatic equalisation.

## Verification

The current tab’s panes use equal dimensions after the action or topology change.

## Recovery

Return to your prior layout if the action is unavailable. Reapply manual sizing only after the last split or close.
