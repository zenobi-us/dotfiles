# Hong Kong is a city of the digitised dead, not an exclusion zone

**Status is `draft` deliberately.** This is RED-era tabletop material rather than
anything read off the game's screen, and the second half of the article is
explicitly labelled inference. Treat it as good background to *ask questions
with*, not as something to state to a player as established.

## What the setting material establishes

Hong Kong was **destroyed by a plague**. It then **regained life** - not as a
rebuilt city but as an **artificial-intelligence society**, formed by Alt
Cunningham's digital consciousness in the remnants of the old NET after the
DataKrash.

The phrase that gets used for it is **"Ghost World"**, and it is meant fairly
literally. It is populated. It is a city *of the digitised dead*, running on the
wreckage of the pre-DataKrash net.

**The common misreading is that it is an exclusion zone** - a quarantined ruin, a
hazard, a place with nothing in it. That inverts the interesting part. The plague
is the beginning of the story, not the end of it, and what is there now is a
functioning society made of people who died.

## Why this is worth having on hand

Because it is one of the few places in the setting where **the thing everyone in
Night City is chasing already exists, for free.** A character with any relationship
to the Blackwall, to rogue AI, to Alt, or to digital afterlife has somewhere real
to point at.

It is also a useful sanity check on a player's premise. "My V wants to reach Hong
Kong" is a coherent ambition with a destination, not a suicide run into a dead
zone.

## The Arasaka angle - this part is inference

**Flagged as reasoning, not documented canon.** It has not been traced to a
source that states it.

Saburo Arasaka's whole apparatus points one direction: **Soulkiller**, **Mikoshi**,
**Secure Your Soul**. That is digital immortality *as corporate property* - your
continued existence held on their hardware, on their terms, as a product.

Ghost World is the free version. And it is curated by **the woman Arasaka
soulkilled**.

If that reading is right, Arasaka's interest in anyone reaching Hong Kong is not
about territory or secrecy - it is that the existence of a functioning free
afterlife is an existential threat to the business model. That is a strong
premise for a character arc.

**But it is a premise, not a fact.** No in-game or tabletop document quoted in
these notes says Arasaka reasons this way. Offer it to a player as an idea, with
the label attached, and let them take it or bin it.

## How to use this in an interview

The good questions here are about the character's relationship to the idea, not
about the geography:

- Does V believe an engram is still the person?
- Would V take Secure Your Soul if it were free? If Arasaka were paying?
- Who does V know who is on a chip somewhere?

Those work whether or not the player ever mentions Hong Kong.
