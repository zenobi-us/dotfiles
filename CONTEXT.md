# Engineering context

This context describes ideas and their conversion into planned work for one repository.

## Language

**Idea**:
A short record of user intent for one repository, before that intent becomes planned work. An idea has an original sentence, context, concepts, and separate exploration suggestions.
_Avoid_: Draft ticket, unfinished initiative

**Concept**:
One identifiable part of an idea that the user wants to pursue. The concept checklist defines the scope that conversion must cover.
_Avoid_: Implementation task, agent suggestion

**Exploration suggestion**:
An agent-proposed direction for future research or grilling. It is not part of conversion scope unless the user approves it as a concept.
_Avoid_: Requirement, confirmed intent

**Research record**:
A separate record of findings and their sources for an idea or selected concepts.

**Grilling record**:
A separate record of questions, answers, and confirmed conclusions for an idea or selected concepts.

**Clarification**:
A question that needs a confirmed resolution through grilling before the affected concept can enter conversion.

**Coverage**:
The user-confirmed relationship between a concept and the initiative or ticket records that cover its intent. Coverage means planned work, not completed implementation.
_Avoid_: Implementation progress

**Conversion**:
The explicit creation or update of initiatives or tickets from an idea after required grilling. Conversion is complete when destination records have confirmed coverage of every concept.
_Avoid_: Implementation, delivery

**Initiative**:
A complete specification that can group executable tickets.
_Avoid_: Specification ticket

**Ticket**:
An executable record of planned work.

**Open idea**:
An idea without confirmed coverage of every concept.

**Done idea**:
An idea whose destination records have confirmed coverage of every concept. Its implementation can remain incomplete.
_Avoid_: Shipped feature

**Archived idea**:
An idea that the user explicitly sets aside without conversion.
